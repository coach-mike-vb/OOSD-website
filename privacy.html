Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Out Of System Digital, LLC, a California limited liability company (“Out Of System Digital,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have, when you use our current and future websites (including outofsystemdigital.com), mobile apps, and related products, features, and services, including, for example, Volleyball Rotations (together, the “Services”).

1. Who our Services are for

Our Services are intended for parents, coaches, and other adults, who may use them to support young athletes. Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at info@outofsystemdigital.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. Parents and coaches who enter information about young athletes into our apps are responsible for having the right to do so.

2. Information we collect

Information you enter in our apps

Our apps let you enter content such as team names, player names, positions, lineups, and rotations. Depending on the app and the features you use, this information may be stored on your device, and in some cases may be transmitted to, stored by, or processed by us or our service providers, for example when you use sharing, backup, sync, or similar features. Each app’s description and in-app disclosures may give more detail about its specific practices.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email or otherwise, we receive your email address and whatever else you choose to include, such as device details or screenshots for a support request.

Information collected automatically

Our website and servers, and our hosting provider, may automatically receive standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages viewed, and the date and time of access. In the future, our apps may collect similar technical and usage information, such as app version, device model, operating system, crash reports, performance data, and how features are used.

Other information we may collect

Depending on the Services and features you use, information we collect may also include: device and advertising identifiers; approximate location derived from your IP address; content and files you create, upload, import, export, or share; purchase, subscription, and transaction information (we do not receive full payment card numbers from Apple); and communications and feedback you send us.

Information from third parties

In the future we may receive information from service providers and partners that help us operate, analyze, and improve the Services.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Services;
  • respond to your questions and provide support;
  • understand how the Services are used, fix bugs, and improve existing and new features;
  • communicate with you about the Services, such as updates, security notices, and changes to our policies;
  • enable features you choose to use, such as sharing;
  • process payments if we introduce paid features;
  • show advertising, if we choose to offer ad-supported features in the future;
  • detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and security issues; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms; and
  • for any other purpose described to you, permitted by law, or for which you give your consent.

4. Sharing and files you choose to share

We may share information in the following ways:

  • At your direction. We may offer features that let a coach export or share a data file, for example with other coaches, parents, and athletes. When you use such a feature, the information in the file, which may include team and player names, goes to the people and services you choose. We do not control what recipients do with it, so please share only with people you trust and only information you have the right to share.
  • Service providers. We may use vendors that help us run the Services, such as hosting, cloud storage, analytics, crash reporting, customer support, and payment processing. They may access information only as needed to perform services for us and are expected to protect it.
  • Advertising partners. If we show ads in the future, ad networks and measurement partners may collect or receive information such as device identifiers, IP address, and general usage information to serve and measure ads, as described in Section 5.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law or legal process, or if we believe it is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • Aggregated or de-identified information. We may share information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

5. Analytics, advertising, and similar technologies

Now or in the future, we and our service providers may use analytics tools, software development kits (SDKs), cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to understand usage and improve the Services. If we offer advertising in the future, we and our advertising partners may use advertising identifiers and similar technologies to show and measure ads, which may include ads based on your interests across other apps and sites (sometimes called “personalized” or “interest-based” advertising).

Where required, we will ask for your permission, for example through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings (on iOS, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Apple Advertising). We do not knowingly direct personalized advertising to users we know to be under 13, and we may limit or change advertising and analytics features as needed to comply with children’s privacy laws. Our Services may not respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals, because there is no common standard for them.

6. Data retention and security

Information stored on your device is generally under your control and may remain there until you delete it or remove the app. For information we or our service providers hold, we keep it for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law, and then delete or de-identify it. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Where available, you can access, edit, and delete information you enter in our apps directly in the app, or by deleting the app. You can also contact us to ask about information we hold about you.

California residents. Under California law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), California residents may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell or share; to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Depending on the features we offer, certain disclosures of personal information, such as to advertising or analytics partners, may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under California law. Where that applies, we will provide opt-out choices as required by law. To make a request, email info@outofsystemdigital.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and you may use an authorized agent where the law permits.

Other regions. Residents of some other U.S. states and countries may have similar rights. Contact us and we will review your request under applicable law.

8. Third-party links and services

Our Services may link to or integrate third-party websites, apps, platforms, and services, including Apple, Google, advertising networks, analytics providers, and other partners. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which are not controlled by this Privacy Policy, and we encourage you to read them.

9. International users

We are based in the United States, and information we hold may be processed and stored in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes to our Services, our practices, or the law. Updates take effect when we post the updated version on this page, as shown by the “Last updated” date above. We may, but are not required to, provide additional notice, except where required by law. Please review this page periodically. Your continued use of the Services after an update means you accept the updated policy.
