Support

Help with Volleyball Rotations and other Out Of System Digital apps.

Contact support

Email us at support@outofsystemdigital.com. Please include your device model, your iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful. We are a small team and do our best to respond as soon as we can.

Frequently asked questions

Is Volleyball Rotations free?

Yes. The app is currently free to use. We may someday charge a nominal fee, which is yet to be determined.

Where is my team and player information stored?

Information you enter, such as team names, rosters, and lineups, is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes that data from your device. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Can I share lineups with other coaches, parents, or players?

Sharing is something we plan to add. If you have ideas about how you would like it to work, we would love to hear them at the email above.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

Please email us. Bug reports and suggestions from coaches, parents, and players directly shape what we build next.

How do I ask about my privacy or data?

See our Privacy Policy or contact us at info@outofsystemdigital.com.
